## How to Work

- Keep everything simple and easy to scan.
- Use tables when they help.
- Link to real evidence instead of writing vague notes.
- Mark something as Done only when the file, screenshot, workflow result, or recording actually exists.
- Keep fixture evidence separate from live or seeded retrieval evidence.
- Do not claim ChromaDB or Ollama is fully working until there is proof.
- Do not include secrets, private course files, or machine-only paths.
